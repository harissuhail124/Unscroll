# Feature-Evidence Matrix (FEATURE_EVIDENCE.md)

This matrix maps every core capability in **Unscroll** to the root causes identified in our student research and connects them directly to survey figures and qualitative interview findings from university students (boys, ages 16–22, Pakistan).

---

| Feature | Part | Root Cause Addressed | Evidence (Survey & Interviews, n = 17) | Safety & Design Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"Before you scroll" Mood Prompt** | **Pause** | Habitual, unconscious phone unlock; opening apps without intentional purpose. | **76%** reported losing track of time scrolling; interviews showed unlocks happen on autopilot during study breaks. | Zero tracking; choice is processed locally and never transmitted to any server. |
| **30-Second Breathing Circle (CSS)** | **Pause** | Stress, elevated heart rate, somatic agitation, and exam anxiety. | **82%** feel worse or drained after social media sessions; students reported difficulty down-regulating nervous tension. | Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` for sensory safety; non-clinical somatic reset. |
| **33-Count Tasbih & "Say Bismillah"** | **Pause** | Spiritual void, agitation, and seeking peace in algorithmic content. | Students explicitly requested faith-based purposeful pauses; 100% preferred gentle remembrance over rigid blockers. | Warm and respectful; optional toggle in Settings allows turning off anytime. |
| **"I'll scroll anyway" Compassionate Response** | **Pause** | Shame, guilt loops, and reactive rebellious scrolling caused by punitive apps. | Students stated punitive apps made them feel like failures, causing them to uninstall the app and binge scroll. | Strictly no shaming, red warnings, or timers counting "wasted hours". |
| **Night Wind-Down Checklist** | **Pause** | Revenge bedtime procrastination, sleep deprivation, and morning lethargy. | **71%** use phone 4+ hours daily, predominantly late at night in bed; students reported waking up drained. | Forgiving checklist with local date reset; no guilt for incomplete items. |
| **Mood-Based Swap Deck (5-min ideas)** | **Swap** | Lack of immediate, appealing, low-friction alternatives to scrolling. | Students cited "having nothing else quick to do" as a primary driver of boredom scrolling. | Ideas require <5 minutes, low executive function, and zero screen equipment. |
| **Daily Purposeful Quests (Move, Learn, Talk)** | **Swap** | Inaction, isolation, and cognitive stagnation during long unstructured university days. | **35%** felt lonely or low; students asked for "small challenges, goals, learning, and self-set limits." | Forgiving design: 3 simple quests; no penalization or broken streaks if uncompleted. |
| **Forgiving 7-Day Active Days Tracker** | **Swap** | Streak anxiety, all-or-nothing mindset, and abandonment after missing 1 day. | Research showed conventional Duolingo/Snapchat streaks induce toxic guilt and app abandonment when broken. | Always shows "X of 7 days active"; progress never drops to 0; no guilt language. |
| **Drain Meter (Fine, Drained, Sleepy, Worse)** | **Reflect** | Lack of post-session self-awareness regarding emotional cost of doom-scrolling. | **82%** report feeling worse or depleted after social media, yet repeat the loop without reflection. | 1-tap private input; builds self-awareness without external judgment or scrutiny. |
| **14-Checkin Proportional Bar Chart** | **Reflect** | Inability to spot emotional patterns across multiple days. | **76%** lose track of hours and cumulative fatigue; interviews highlighted unawareness of mental depletion. | Local in-browser summary; no scoring, no user ranking, and no parent/staff dashboard. |
| **Reality Check Reminders (Social Comparison)** | **Reflect** | FOMO, perceived inadequacy, and toxic comparison to online highlight reels. | **35%** reported feeling low or lonely directly after seeing peers' reels or posts. | Grounding text cards reminding students that posts are curated highlights, not real life. |
| **My Wins / Shukr Gratitude List** | **Reflect** | Negative cognitive bias, feelings of daily unproductivity, and low self-esteem. | Students felt guilt over "wasting entire days" even when they had studied or helped family. | Private on-device diary. If distress is entered, UI stays calm and keeps Help Hub visible. |
| **Reach Out Copy-to-Clipboard Templates** | **Reach** | Social friction, loneliness, and awkwardness initiating real-world contact. | **35%** reported feeling low or lonely, yet struggled to message friends face-to-face. | Pre-drafted low-pressure texts (chai, study, walk); copies locally to native clipboard. |
| **Weekly Offline Challenge** | **Reach** | Disconnection from physical environment and campus community. | Students reported desire for real-world interactions without feeling monitored. | One single checkable card per week; zero proof-of-work photos required. |
| **Help Hub & Campus Counselor Field** | **Reach / Global** | Acute distress, depression, and severe isolation. | Only **6%** chose a counselor as support they would actively seek due to stigma and friction. | Stored 100% on-device; placeholder `[Add campus counselor contact]`; non-medical disclaimer. |
| **On-Device Karachi Prayer Calculations** | **Faith / Global** | Losing track of prayer times due to hypnotic app rabbit holes. | Students asked for gentle Deen anchors to structure their day around Salah rather than feeds. | Computed via `adhan` strictly on-device; zero GPS coordinates leave the phone. |
