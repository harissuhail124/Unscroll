# Unscroll: Student User Guide (USER_GUIDE.md)

Welcome to **Unscroll**—your gentle, private companion to pause mindless doom-scrolling, recharge your energy, and reclaim your time for what actually matters at university.

---

## 1. How to Install Unscroll on Your Phone (PWA)

Unscroll is an installable web app that runs fast, works offline, and takes almost zero storage.

### On Android (Chrome or Edge):
1. Open the Unscroll link in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Tap the three dots menu (**⋮**) in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Add**. Unscroll will now appear on your home screen like any normal app and will open full-screen without browser bars.

### On iPhone (Safari):
1. Open the Unscroll link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share button** (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right. Unscroll is now on your home screen!

## 2. The Four Tabs Explained in Plain Words

Navigate between the four main sections using the bottom text bar:

### 1. Pause (Break the Habit Loop)
* **Before You Scroll**: When you feel the urge to unlock your phone and scroll, open Pause first. Tap what is pulling you: *Bored, Stressed, Lonely, Missing out, or Just habit*.
* **30-Second Breath**: Follow the calm expanding and contracting circle for 30 seconds to let your heart rate settle. If Faith features are ON, you can also do a quick 33-count tasbih (*SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar*).
* **Your Choice**: After the pause, tap **"Swap it"** to try a purposeful 5-minute activity, or tap **"I'll scroll anyway"** if you need to check your phone. No judgment, no shame.
* **Night Wind-Down Checklist**: Protect your sleep before bed: plug your phone across the room, write one task for tomorrow, and take slow breaths.

### 2. Swap (Purposeful Replacements)
* **5-Minute Swap Deck**: Replaces the scroll urge with one simple, fun, or relaxing action based on your mood (solve a chess puzzle, do a neck stretch, splash cold water, or read 1 book page). Tap **"Another idea"** to cycle through more options!
* **Daily Quests**: Three forgiving goals for your day: *Move 10 mins*, *Learn 10 mins*, and *Talk to someone in person*.
* **Forgiving Progress**: Shows how many of the last 7 days you paused or completed a quest. It never resets to zero and never scolds you if you miss a day.

### 3. Reflect (Self-Awareness & Peace of Mind)
* **Drain Meter**: Right after putting your phone down, tap how you feel: *Fine, Drained, Sleepy, or Worse*.
* **14 Check-In Summary**: A clean colored bar shows your pattern over your last 14 sessions so you can see which apps drain you most.
* **Perspective Check**: Short, punchy reminders dismantling social comparison (e.g., *"Posts are highlights, not whole lives"*).
* **My Wins / Shukr**: A private on-device list where you write one good thing that happened today.

### 4. Reach (Real Human Connection)
* **Reach Out Templates**: Quick text messages you can copy with one tap and send to a classmate or friend on WhatsApp/SMS (e.g. *"Free for a walk or tea today?"*).
* **Weekly Offline Challenge**: A small weekly challenge to enjoy real life without a screen (e.g., sharing chai with phones in your bags).
* **Help Hub**: If you are ever feeling overwhelmed, low, or unsafe, tap the Help Hub. You can save your campus counselor's room or office details right on your phone.

---

## 3. Faith Features (Optional Deen Integration)
* **Prayer Times**: Shows the next prayer time calculated directly on your phone using the Karachi method.
* **Sunnah Adhkar & Verses**: Verified morning, evening, and bedtime adhkar, along with a daily Qur'an verse and an educational Deen quiz.
* **Turn On or Off Anytime**: You can turn Faith features off or on in **Settings** (top right) whenever you wish.

---

## 4. Privacy Guarantee
Everything in Unscroll stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no logins, and nobody (professors, parents, or algorithms) is watching you.

*Note: Unscroll is a personal habit tool. Unscroll is not a counselor or medical service.*
